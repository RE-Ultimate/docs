You do not need to build every machine. Start with a small processing line, add reliable power and storage, then expand when a recipe or project needs it.
These routes are suggestions, not a required order. The [block reference](/docs/blocks/) covers selected useful blocks from the pack.

## First processing and power

Choose a processing route that fits your available materials:

- **IC2:** a [Generator](/docs/blocks/ic2/generator/), [Macerator](/docs/blocks/ic2/macerator/), and [Electric Furnace](/docs/blocks/ic2/electric-furnace/) form an EU-powered ore-processing line. A [BatBox](/docs/blocks/ic2/batbox/) stores power between jobs.
- **Thermal Expansion:** a [Pulverizer](/docs/blocks/thermal-expansion/pulverizer/) and [Powered Furnace](/docs/blocks/thermal-expansion/powered-furnace/) offer an MJ-powered route. A [Magmatic Engine](/docs/blocks/thermal-expansion/magmatic-engine/) can supply power when you have a lava source.

EU and MJ are different systems. Match each machine to a supported supply; do not connect a higher-voltage IC2 storage block directly to a basic machine. The machine pages describe their limits.
Use EMI to compare the installed recipes and processing results before spending scarce materials.

## Storage and item movement

A [Barrel](/docs/blocks/factorization/barrel/) is useful for a large amount of one item. When finding items across many inventories becomes slow, build an AE1 network with an [ME Controller](/docs/blocks/applied-energistics/me-controller/), [ME Drive](/docs/blocks/applied-energistics/me-drive/), and [ME Access Terminal](/docs/blocks/applied-energistics/me-access-terminal/).

Add [Import](/docs/blocks/applied-energistics/me-import-bus/) and [Export Buses](/docs/blocks/applied-energistics/me-export-bus/) as machines need automatic supply and collection. An [ME Storage Bus](/docs/blocks/applied-energistics/me-storage-bus/) gives the network access to an existing inventory.

For an external item line, start with a [Wooden Transport Pipe](/docs/blocks/buildcraft/wooden-transport-pipe/) or a RedPower [Filter](/docs/blocks/redpower2/filter/). More complex tube systems can use a [Sorting Machine](/docs/blocks/redpower2/sorting-machine/). Check the destination and output capacity before adding more machines.

## Farms and repeatable supplies

Pair an MFR [Planter](/docs/blocks/minefactory-reloaded/planter/) with a [Harvester](/docs/blocks/minefactory-reloaded/harvester/) for supported crops and trees. Align their work areas and collect the outputs. Add a [Fertilizer](/docs/blocks/minefactory-reloaded/fertilizer/) when growth is the limiting step.

Forestry provides another useful processing route. The [Fermenter](/docs/blocks/forestry/fermenter/) and [Still](/docs/blocks/forestry/still/) process biomass and biofuel. Bees are an optional longer project: begin with an [Apiary](/docs/blocks/forestry/apiary/) and use a [Centrifuge](/docs/blocks/forestry/centrifuge/) for supported combs.

## Steel, steam, and advanced materials

A Railcraft [Coke Oven](/docs/blocks/railcraft/coke-oven/) and [Blast Furnace](/docs/blocks/railcraft/blast-furnace/) provide a fuel and steel route. Consider a [Steam Boiler](/docs/blocks/railcraft/steam-boiler/) when you have a steady fuel supply, water supply, and use for steam.

GregTech adds separate processing machines. The [Industrial Centrifuge](/docs/blocks/gregtech/industrial-centrifuge/) and [Industrial Electrolyzer](/docs/blocks/gregtech/industrial-electrolyzer/) recover materials from supported inputs. Build the [Industrial Blast Furnace](/docs/blocks/gregtech/industrial-blast-furnace/), [Industrial Grinder](/docs/blocks/gregtech/industrial-grinder/), or [Vacuum Freezer](/docs/blocks/gregtech/vacuum-freezer/) when a needed material calls for that process. These use the legacy GregTech 2 structures, not layouts from later versions.

## Remote work and optional projects

A [Quarry](/docs/blocks/buildcraft/quarry/) or [Mining Turtle](/docs/blocks/computercraft/mining-turtle/) can collect resources away from your base. Plan power or fuel, output storage, and chunk loading. An [Ender Chest](/docs/blocks/ender-storage/ender-chest/) shares an item inventory between locations; an [Ender Tank](/docs/blocks/ender-storage/ender-tank/) shares liquid storage. Neither powers the machines at the other end.

For Thaumcraft, begin with the [Research Table](/docs/blocks/thaumcraft/research-table/) and [Arcane Worktable](/docs/blocks/thaumcraft/arcane-worktable/). The [Crucible](/docs/blocks/thaumcraft/crucible/) and [Infusion Altar](/docs/blocks/thaumcraft/infusion-altar/) open further crafting. Follow the Thaumcraft 3 entries in your Thaumonomicon.
