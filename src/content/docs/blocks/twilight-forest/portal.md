The Twilight Forest Portal connects the Overworld to the Twilight Forest dimension. Build it outdoors when you are ready to explore and want a way back to your base.

## Build the pool

Make a two by two pool of water, one block deep, with solid blocks below all four water blocks. Surround the pool at water level with a complete one-block-wide ring of grass or dirt. This ring has twelve blocks, including the four corners.

Place flowers on every block of the ring. The code also accepts other plant or leaf materials, but flowers give a clear way to check each position. All twelve positions must have a suitable block above the grass or dirt. An empty corner is enough to prevent creation.

Throw a Diamond into the water. When the game finds a valid pool near the dropped Diamond, lightning strikes and the four water blocks become portal blocks. Keep the pool and its ring intact if the portal does not form, then check the foundation and each plant position. Portal creation is checked periodically, so it may not happen at once.

## Travel

Step into the portal to travel to the Twilight Forest. Use the portal on the other side to return. Keep the surrounding ground in place: removing supporting grass or dirt next to the active portal can break portal blocks. The pack permits players to create portals without operator status, but does not enable creation in arbitrary other dimensions.
