The Glacial Precipitator turns water into snowballs, snow blocks, or ice. It needs MJ and a water supply. Use it when you need a steady supply without collecting snow or ice by hand.

## Set up

Connect an MJ source and supply water to a configured input face. An [Aqueous Accumulator](/docs/blocks/thermal-expansion/aqueous-accumulator/) can provide water. Open the window and select one of the three products. The lower slot holds the result. Remove the output or configure an output face to send it to an adjacent inventory.

Each operation consumes water. Four snowballs or one snow block use 500 mB; one ice block uses 1,000 mB. The machine also consumes energy for each operation. Keep the water tank and energy supply filled to continue production.

## Check a stopped machine

Check the selected product, water and energy gauges, output space, and redstone setting. An incompatible item in the output slot prevents a new product from entering it. Empty the slot before changing products if needed. The side configuration controls which faces accept liquids and send items.
