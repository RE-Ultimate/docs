The Barrel holds a large amount of one matching item type in a compact block. It is useful for cobblestone, crops and other bulk outputs. Check EMI for the installed recipe.

## Use

Use the Barrel with an item in hand to add matching items. A quick second use can add matching items from the inventory. Hit the Barrel to take a stack; sneak and hit it to take one item. An empty Barrel can accept a new item type after its contents are removed.

The normal capacity is 4,096 items. A Barrel enlargement upgrade raises the capacity to 65,536 items. The display identifies the stored item and quantity, making a row of Barrels useful for a simple bulk store.

For automation, the top and bottom expose separate inventory slots; the horizontal sides do not expose slots. Connect insertion above and extraction below. Keep different item types in separate Barrels and check the route before sending a mixed farm output.
