The Writing Desk is a Mystcraft workstation for handling symbols and books. Use it to organize pages and copy symbols into a notebook or an Age book. It needs a two-block space when placed.

## Prepare the desk

Place the desk and open it. The window has a book slot, paper slot, ink container slot, and a set of notebook slots. Put a notebook or a compatible book in the book slot. Put paper in the paper slot and supply ink through the ink container slot. Empty containers go to the adjacent output slot.

Use the notebook slots to keep books available while you work. The left side of the window displays pages and the right side displays the selected book. You can move between pages and select a symbol to write. Writing uses ink. Copying symbols into a notebook also needs paper, so check both supplies if the desk cannot write.

## Work with books

The desk can name notebooks and linking books. It can also name an Age through its book. Use clear names to distinguish destinations and notebooks before you travel. Keep pages you want to reuse in a notebook, and check your selected book before writing into it.

The desk is a writing tool, not a guarantee that a newly written Age will be stable. Keep a way back with you when you visit an Age. Use EMI to check the recipes for paper, ink, and other Mystcraft supplies.
