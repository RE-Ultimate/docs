The Centrifuge processes bee products into materials such as beeswax and honey drops. It is a useful companion to an [Apiary](/docs/blocks/forestry/apiary/) once the first combs start to accumulate.

## Setup and use

Connect BuildCraft-compatible MJ power and put a supported item in the input slot. Take the processed items from the output slots, or arrange item transport to remove them. Use EMI to check which combs or other materials the installed pack accepts.

Different combs have different products. Some outputs have a chance to appear, so a single comb does not guarantee every listed item. Beeswax is useful in Forestry crafting; honey drops can be processed further in a [Squeezer](/docs/blocks/forestry/squeezer/).

Keep output slots clear, especially when a recipe can yield several item types. If work stops, check the input, the available output space and the power supply. The Centrifuge does not house bees; the Apiary produces the combs and the Centrifuge processes them.
