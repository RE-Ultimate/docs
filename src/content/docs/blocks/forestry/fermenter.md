The Fermenter turns plant material into biomass. It is the first processing step in a renewable liquid fuel line. For a basic setup, use saplings from a tree farm and send the biomass to a [Still](/docs/blocks/forestry/still/).

## Setup and use

Supply BuildCraft-compatible MJ power, a valid plant input, a liquid and fermenter fuel. Vanilla saplings, wheat, sugar cane and cactus are among the registered plant inputs. Water works as a liquid input. Juice is also accepted by the standard plant recipes and has a higher output multiplier.

The fermenter fuel is a separate input from the plant material and machine power. Forestry fertilizer, compost and mulch are registered fuels. Apples processed in a [Squeezer](/docs/blocks/forestry/squeezer/) can provide juice and sometimes mulch.

Remove biomass from the product tank with a compatible container or liquid connection. Keep the output tank from filling, and replenish all three inputs. A machine with power alone cannot ferment without plant material, a suitable liquid and fermenter fuel. Check EMI for the available recipes and ingredients.

The Fermenter also supports a mead process using honeydew and honey. This is a separate recipe from the plant-material biomass route.
