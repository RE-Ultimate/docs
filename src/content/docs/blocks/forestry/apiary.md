The Apiary houses bees for breeding and production. Use it to start collecting combs and to learn which conditions a species needs.

## Start a colony

Place a princess and a drone in their matching slots. They form a queen, which works until its life ends. Collect the offspring and products from the output slots, then place a princess and drone back in the input slots to start another cycle. Different parents can produce different offspring; keep spare bees while experimenting.

Bees need suitable surroundings. The required climate, flowers, time and weather depend on their traits. If a queen does not work, check the interface for its condition and move the Apiary or supply the required flowers. A Beealyzer helps inspect bee traits.

The Apiary has slots for frames. Frames affect bee operation and wear out; they are optional for starting a colony. Process combs in a powered [Centrifuge](/docs/blocks/forestry/centrifuge/). Do not expect each queen to produce every possible specialty or mutation.
