The Thermionic Fabricator makes electron tubes from molten glass and other ingredients. Tubes are useful for Forestry circuit boards and machine upgrades.

## Setup and use

Connect BuildCraft-compatible MJ power and put glass in the melting input. Wait for the machine to heat and melt it. Place the other ingredients in the crafting grid, then take the result. Use EMI for the tube recipe you need.

The fabricator also accepts glass panes and sand as melting inputs. Sand needs more heat than glass. Its molten material cools if the machine loses heat, so keep it powered while preparing a batch.

The machine has a supply inventory as well as the crafting grid. Check the glass level, heat, ingredient layout and power if the result is unavailable. Some recipes need an additional cast; check the specific recipe rather than assuming all fabricator recipes use the same inputs.
