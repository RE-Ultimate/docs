The Carpenter makes items that need a liquid as well as crafting ingredients. It is an early Forestry machine: for example, it makes impregnated casings for bee equipment and circuit boards for other machines.

## Setup and use

Supply BuildCraft-compatible MJ power. Open the interface and set the recipe in the crafting grid. Supply the matching ingredients and the liquid specified by the recipe. Some recipes also require an item in the separate container slot. Use EMI to check the installed recipe and the required liquid.

The machine stores the liquid in its tank. Add it with a compatible filled container or a liquid connection. It also has space for supplies and a finished product. Keep output space free so that completed items can leave the machine.

For an impregnated casing, make seed oil in a [Squeezer](/docs/blocks/forestry/squeezer/) and supply it to the Carpenter. Water is used for many other recipes, but it does not replace seed oil in that casing recipe. If the machine does not start, check the grid, supply slots, tank contents and power.
