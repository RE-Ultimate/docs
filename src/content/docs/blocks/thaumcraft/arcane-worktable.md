The Arcane Worktable is an early crafting station for Thaumcraft recipes that need vis. It can also handle ordinary crafting recipes.

## Setup and use

Place a Table and right-click it with a wand to make the worktable. Open it and arrange ingredients in the crafting grid. For an arcane recipe, put a casting wand in the bottom-right wand slot. The recipe draws vis from that wand.

If an arcane recipe does not complete, check that the wand has enough vis and that you have learned the required research. Wands slowly recharge from nearby aura nodes while carried. Use EMI and the Thaumonomicon to check the installed recipe and research requirements.

The [Infusion Altar](/docs/blocks/thaumcraft/infusion-altar/) can perform worktable recipes too, as well as recipes that need aspects from a nearby source. The worktable is still useful before you reach that stage.
