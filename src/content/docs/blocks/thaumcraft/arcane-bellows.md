The Arcane Bellows provides a continuous stream of air to compatible blocks. It is useful when a Thaumcraft workshop needs faster processing.

## Use

Research the bellows and check its infusion recipe in the Thaumonomicon or EMI. Attach it to the machine you want to improve.

On an [Infernal Furnace](/docs/blocks/thaumcraft/infernal-furnace/), the bellows increases smelting speed, reduces Flux, and improves the chance of bonus materials. It can also increase the speed of a normal furnace. On a [Crucible](/docs/blocks/thaumcraft/crucible/), it reduces the time needed for water to boil.

The bellows runs by itself. Its effects depend on the connected block: it does not replace the Crucible's heat source or the Infernal Furnace's supply of local vis.
