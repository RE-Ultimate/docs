The Infusion Altar extends arcane crafting with recipes that consume aspects from nearby sources. It also handles ordinary and arcane worktable recipes.

## Setup and use

Research Magical Building Blocks and consult the Thaumonomicon for its construction diagram. Place four Arcane Stone Blocks as shown and use a wand to form the altar. Check EMI for the installed Arcane Stone Block recipe.

Put the recipe components in the altar. Its interface shows the required aspects. A nearby boiling [Crucible](/docs/blocks/thaumcraft/crucible/) can supply them. An Arcane Alembic or a filled Warded Jar can also serve as a source.

Each aspect must come from one source with enough of that aspect. The altar cannot combine the same aspect from multiple sources to reach the required amount. Different aspects can come from different sources.

A Crucible may spill leftover aspects as Flux after the altar draws from it. When shift-clicking to craft multiples, the in-game guide says this spill occurs at the end of the batch. This altar uses Thaumcraft 3 mechanics; later infusion layouts are different.
