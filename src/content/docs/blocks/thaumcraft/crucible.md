The Crucible breaks items into their component aspects. Use it for alchemy and as an aspect source for an [Infusion Altar](/docs/blocks/thaumcraft/infusion-altar/).

## Setup and use

Place a cauldron and use a wand on it to make a Crucible. Put a constant heat source underneath, fill it with water, and wait for it to boil. Drop ingredients into the boiling water to break them down. Use the wand on the Crucible to attempt an alchemical recipe. Check the Thaumonomicon and EMI for the recipe available in your installation.

The amounts and types of aspects matter. Extra aspects can escape into the local aura as Flux when you craft. Plan the ingredients to limit the leftovers. Sneak and use the wand to empty the remaining aspects into the aura without attempting a recipe.

Carry paper and Scribing Tools when experimenting: the in-game guide says experiments can lead to discoveries. An Arcane Bellows can reduce the time needed for the water to boil.
