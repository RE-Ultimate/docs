The GraviChestPlate is powered chest armor with a flight mode. It combines protection and mobility in one chest slot, making it useful for construction and travel after you have a strong IC2 power supply. It holds up to 10 million EU.

## Use

Charge the chestplate in a compatible IC2 charging machine and equip it in the chest armor slot. Press the **Gravi Fly Key** to turn its flight mode on or off. The default binding is F; check the controls menu if another mod uses the same key. The suit needs at least 80,000 EU to enable flight.

Flight consumes stored energy. Watch the charge display and land before the battery runs low. The boost control increases movement speed and energy use. The chestplate also uses energy to absorb damage, so a fight can reduce the energy available for the return trip. Carry a way to recharge it when travelling far.

## Materials

The recipe combines a QuantumSuit Bodyarmor and an Ultimate Lappack with two Gravitation Engines, a high-voltage transformer and superconductors. The superconductors use the ore dictionary, so check EMI for accepted inputs. The pack keeps the QuantumSuit body armor recipe enabled; its other IC2 ingredients can depend on pack settings. Check the current recipes in EMI before making the components.
