The Cart Assembler makes modular minecarts. Pick a hull and add compatible modules to give the cart an engine, tools, storage, or other functions. The hull limits the space and complexity available for modules.

## Assemble a cart

Place the assembler and open its window. Put a cart hull in the hull slot. The window enables the module slots that the selected hull supports. Add the modules you want and read the status messages before starting. The assembler reports conflicts and other problems in the window. A valid design shows that it is ready to assemble.

Add fuel to the assembler's fuel slot and start assembly. The progress display shows the remaining work. When it finishes, take the cart from the output slot. Assembly fuel powers the assembler; the finished cart may need its own fuel or other supplies for its engine and tools. Check the module descriptions before you send a cart out to work.

## Plan the modules

A larger hull can make room for more equipment, but module compatibility and the complexity limit still matter. Add an engine that suits the intended job and a tool for the task. Storage modules give the cart room to hold items it collects. The status panel helps identify an invalid combination before you spend time assembling it.

Keep the output slot clear. If you change the hull, remove modules from slots that the replacement cannot use. Check EMI for the installed hull and module recipes.
