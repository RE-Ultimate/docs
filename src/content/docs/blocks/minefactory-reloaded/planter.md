The Planter automates planting when you supply compatible seeds or saplings. Pair it with a [Harvester](/docs/blocks/minefactory-reloaded/harvester/) to make a renewable farm.

## Setup

Place the Planter below the center of the farm soil, with the soil directly above it and space for plants above the soil. Its default area is 3 by 3. The Planter checks the planting conditions for each item, so use suitable soil and light for the plant.

Supply BuildCraft MJ or IC2 EU and put planting items in its inventory. Pipes can access the nine planting slots from any side. A redstone signal stops the machine. A range upgrade increases the planting area.

The nine inventory slots correspond to parts of the field when the multi-plant setting is enabled. This helps arrange different crops, but the machine can try other slots when the preferred item cannot be planted. Return seeds from the Harvester to keep the farm supplied.
