The Rancher gathers products from supported animals. It is useful for a steady supply from a contained herd. Check EMI for the installed recipe.

## Setup

Face the Rancher into an animal pen and provide BuildCraft MJ or IC2 EU. Its default horizontal work area is 5 by 5 in front of the machine; a range upgrade expands it. Keep adult animals inside the area and leave room for their normal behavior.

The installed handlers include cows, mooshrooms, sheep and squid. Their products differ: the Rancher can collect items as well as liquids. Connect an adjacent compatible inventory or item pipe for item drops, and a compatible tank or liquid pipe for liquids. The internal liquid tank holds 4,000 mB and pushes to adjacent compatible tanks.

The Rancher stores liquid products directly rather than requiring a supply of empty buckets. It stops when powered by redstone. If output is missing, check the animal type, work area, power and output connections.
