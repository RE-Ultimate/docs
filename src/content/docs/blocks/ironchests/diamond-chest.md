The Diamond Chest holds 108 stacks in one block. Its window has nine rows of twelve slots. It is part of the Iron Chests storage family, which provides larger single-block inventories than a normal chest.

## Chest sizes

A Copper Chest holds 45 stacks, an Iron Chest 54, a Silver Chest 72, and a Gold Chest 81. Diamond, Crystal, and Obsidian Chests each hold 108. Crystal Chests show some of their contents through their sides. Obsidian Chests resist explosions. The three largest types have the same inventory capacity, so choose Crystal or Obsidian for its other property rather than extra space.

The installed recipes offer more than one path through the tiers. For example, you can make a Diamond Chest from a Gold Chest, diamonds, and glass. There is also a route using a Silver Chest. Check EMI for the exact recipes and available ore-dictionary materials in this pack.

## Upgrading in place

Chest upgrade items change a compatible chest to the indicated type. Use the upgrade on the chest you want to change. The item names show both the starting and target tiers, such as Iron to Gold Chest Upgrade. Other upgrades lead from a Diamond Chest to Crystal or Obsidian. An upgrade applies only to its matching starting chest, so check the item name before you use it.

Chests keep their inventory when upgraded. Allow room to open the chest and collect items from its window. A larger chest gives more slots in the same footprint, but does not combine with a neighboring chest to make a double chest.
