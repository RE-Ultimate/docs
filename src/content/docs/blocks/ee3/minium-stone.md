The Minium Stone is a reusable tool for changing materials through EE3 transmutation recipes. It also gives you a portable crafting grid. The stone takes damage as you use it, so keep an eye on its durability and prepare a replacement.

## Getting the stone

Craft an Inert Stone, then surround it with eight Shards of Minium in a crafting grid. The shards come from hostile creatures. The finished stone does not stack, so each stone has its own remaining durability.

## Using transmutation

Place the stone and the required materials in a crafting grid. Take the result and the stone remains in the grid with additional damage. For example, the installed EE3 code registers a conversion from eight Iron Ingots to one Gold Ingot. It also registers the reverse conversion. Check EMI for the available recipes and their quantities before spending materials.

The stone can also work on supported blocks in the world. Select the desired result with the EE3 toggle key while targeting a valid block, then use the stone on it. The controls can be changed in the game settings. Test the result on a small area before converting blocks you need to keep.

## Portable grid

Hold the stone and use the EE3 extra key to open its crafting window. The pack's default is C; another mod or a changed key binding can affect this. This window uses the normal crafting grid, so the stone can stay in your inventory until a recipe needs it.
