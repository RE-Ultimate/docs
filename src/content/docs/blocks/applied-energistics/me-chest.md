An ME Chest gives direct access to a single storage cell. It is a useful first step into ME storage or a compact way to inspect a cell without a full terminal and drive setup.

## Set up

Supply the chest with compatible power and insert a storage cell in its cell slot. Open the chest to access that cell's contents. The chest can also connect by ME Cable to an [ME Controller](/docs/blocks/applied-energistics/me-controller/), which supplies network power and makes the cell available to the network.

Items can be inserted into the chest by external equipment. For access to several cells through one screen, use an [ME Drive](/docs/blocks/applied-energistics/me-drive/) and an [ME Access Terminal](/docs/blocks/applied-energistics/me-access-terminal/). The chest holds only one cell, and the cell has limits on both item types and total contents.

If the chest does not work, check its power and confirm that a cell is installed. Search EMI for the installed chest and cell recipes.
